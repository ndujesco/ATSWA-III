Keep the exam technique. Check the law that supplies the numbers.

The 15 INSIGHT diets in this library run from March 2019 to March 2026. Their questions and official solutions use the law applicable to those papers. The 2026 study text uses the new Nigeria Tax Act (NTA) and Nigeria Tax Administration Act (NTAA). A correct historical answer can therefore differ from a correct current-law answer.

Two answers can belong to two different periods. The site preserves the printed question, official answer and supplied tax tables. A “Check this” note explains a changed rule or a source defect. A reviewed study answer is labelled separately when the printed key itself is defective.

How to practise an older paper

  1. Identify the period. Read the dates in the requirement. Use the supplied “Exam tables” when reconstructing that original answer.
  2. Attempt the method. Separate employment income, business adjustments, deductions, capital allowances and the tax calculation. Show your workings before checking the source solution.
  3. Read the question’s flag. Follow its primary-law reference and its chapter/section link. Some old provisions have a replacement; some have no direct current equivalent.
  4. Rework the changed part for 2026. State the current assumptions and rules. Changing a rate alone is insufficient if the base, relief or exemption also changed.

One salary, two computations

Assume annual gross employment income of ₦4,800,000, actual annual rent of ₦1,200,000, and no other eligible deductions or exempt benefits. This compares an ordinary old-CRA computation with a current NTA computation; it does not apply both sets of relief to the same year.

StepOld CRA frameworkCurrent NTA framework
Gross income₦4,800,000₦4,800,000
Relief in this example₦200,000 + 20% × ₦4,800,000 = ₦1,160,000 CRA20% × ₦1,200,000 = ₦240,000 rent relief
Chargeable income₦3,640,000₦4,560,000
Band calculation₦300k × 7% + ₦300k × 11% + ₦500k × 15% + ₦500k × 19% + ₦1.6m × 21% + ₦440k × 24%₦800k × 0% + ₦2.2m × 15% + ₦1.56m × 18%
Annual tax₦665,600₦610,800

The current chargeable income is higher in this example because CRA is gone, while the current tax is lower because the bands changed. This is why copying just the old taxable income into new rates gives an unreliable comparison. Use the full computation format and state your assumptions.
